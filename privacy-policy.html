3. Information Curnex Collects

Curnex is designed to operate without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information for its basic currency conversion functionality.

Depending on how you use the App, Curnex may process or store the following types of information:

  • Currency selections.
  • Amounts entered for currency conversion.
  • Exchange-rate information.
  • Exchange-rate dates.
  • Exchange-rate update timestamps.
  • Information required to provide offline functionality.
  • App settings or preferences, where applicable.

The basic currency conversion calculations are performed using information necessary to provide the requested conversion.

4. Information You Enter

When you enter an amount into Curnex for currency conversion, the amount is used to calculate the corresponding converted value.

Curnex does not require you to provide your name, phone number, home address, or other personal information in order to use its basic currency conversion functionality.

You should not enter sensitive personal information into currency conversion fields or other areas of the App that are not specifically designed to collect such information.

5. Exchange-Rate Information

Curnex uses exchange-rate information to perform currency conversions.

Exchange-rate information may be retrieved from third-party exchange-rate providers or other external data sources.

Curnex may save previously retrieved exchange-rate information so that the App can continue to provide conversion functionality when an Internet connection is unavailable.

Saved exchange-rate information may include the currency pair, exchange rate, rate date, and the time at which the information was retrieved or updated.

6. Offline Functionality

Curnex may provide offline currency conversion using exchange rates previously retrieved while an Internet connection was available.

When offline functionality is used, Curnex may access exchange rates stored locally on your device.

Offline exchange rates may become outdated because currency exchange rates can change over time.

7. Local Storage

Curnex may store certain information locally on your device in order to improve functionality and performance.

Locally stored information may include exchange-rate data, timestamps, application preferences, or other information necessary for the operation of Curnex.

Information stored locally generally remains on your device unless it is removed by you, automatically removed by the App, or otherwise deleted through the operating system or application settings.

8. Internet Connection

Curnex may require an Internet connection to retrieve updated exchange-rate information.

The App may check the availability of a network connection before attempting to retrieve updated information.

If an Internet connection is unavailable, Curnex may use previously stored exchange-rate information where available.
